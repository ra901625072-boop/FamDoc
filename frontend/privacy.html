Official Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026 • Version 1.2

Family Document Management System (FDMS / "FamDoc") is designed and operated to safeguard the privacy, confidentiality, and security of you and your family members. This Privacy Policy details how our application collects, uses, protects, and isolates information when you access the platform and connect storage services, including Google Drive.

1. Information We Collect

To provide private family document archival, FamDoc processes only the minimum necessary data:

  • Account Details: Basic profile credentials including username and email address supplied during family vault registration.
  • Connected Storage Identifiers: When connecting Google Drive, we store your Google account email address and drive identifier strictly to display your connected storage in your family roster.
  • Document Metadata: File names, file sizes, MIME types, document categories, and upload timestamps needed to organize your family vault.

2. Google Drive API Usage & Strict Data Isolation

FamDoc connects to the Google Drive API exclusively under the restricted drive-file permission scope:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Strict App Isolation Guarantee: Under the drive.file scope, FamDoc can only access, view, or manage files and folders created or uploaded directly through FamDoc. FamDoc has zero technical ability to see, read, modify, or delete your existing personal Google Drive files, Google Photos, Gmail, or documents uploaded by other apps.

  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, monetize, rent, or trade your documents, family records, or personal Google account data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No Artificial Intelligence Training: Your family records and files are never used to train public machine learning or AI models.
  • Storage Pooling Only: The Google Drive API is utilized solely to provide secure encrypted storage for family keepsakes and calculate quota utilization.

3. Data Retention & Revocation of Access

You maintain full ownership and control over your family documents at all times:

  • You may disconnect your Google Drive at any time from the Cloud Storage management page.
  • Upon disconnection, OAuth access and refresh tokens are immediately and irreversibly purged from our database.
  • Files placed in the Recycle Bin are retained for 30 days for accidental deletion recovery and can be permanently purged at any time.

4. Encryption & Security Architecture

FamDoc enforces end-to-end security protocols across all application layers:

  • All network traffic between your browser, mobile app, and backend APIs is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.
  • Storage authorization credentials and tokens are encrypted at rest in the database using modern cryptographic standards.
  • Previews and file streaming utilize ephemeral single-use verification tokens.
